Methodology

{{ m.note }} The risk score is a sum of the contributions of the signals below (at most 100). It is computed once, at launch time, from what was known then and from the launch transaction itself, and then frozen with the configuration version. What the dev and the buyers do after the launch is shown on the launch page as facts; it never changes the score.

Early buyers and the links between them

The launch page lists the first buyers of a launch and, for each, the links we can show with a transaction. They are facts about transfers and trades; they are not part of the score, and they do not say who controls a wallet.

  • dev side: the wallet received NEAR from the dev, from a wallet linked to the dev, or from the source that funded the dev, by a direct transfer in the days before its first buy.
  • bundle: several wallets bought within the first seconds of the launch and had been funded by the same narrow source shortly before it.
  • pair: two wallets whose first buys of the same launch came within seconds of each other in several launches. A pair is shown with the transactions of both buys, also when one of the two buys almost every launch.
  • group: three or more wallets in which every two form a pair. A wallet that is an early buyer in most of the launches of its active period is put in no group and links no other wallet to one; there is no chaining through a third wallet, and a wallet is in at most one group.

Service accounts (launchpads, bots, exchanges, relayers) are listed explicitly and are never a source or a member; no account is included or excluded by the look of its name. The funding of a wallet is loaded for the days before its first buy, so "none found" means that our data shows none. The thresholds are configuration values and may change.
