Accuracy how the risk forecasts did

{% set s = a.summary %}

What is counted. Only our live forecasts: a launch is counted if it received a risk card when it launched, and only once its 24-hour outcome is final. Launches from before the cards existed, and history we loaded later, are never counted. A rug is defined on the methodology page: the insiders sold into the other buyers within 24 hours and those buyers lost.

The live score is not calibrated yet. The weights are provisional (v0), a backtest on history is further down: a score of 70 does not mean a 70% chance. Compare a band's rug rate with the base rate, not with the score. Most launches fade anyway, so a raw hit rate means nothing without the base rate. Counts are small, so every rate comes with an interval; rows with fewer than {{ a.min_n }} launches are greyed. Dev exit and Buyers' loss are the share of launches with those secondary facts (defined on the methodology page).
